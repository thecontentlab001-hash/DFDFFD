Common Anxieties,
Answered.

We understand the fear that comes with pediatric surgery. Clarity is the antidote to anxiety.

Will the surgery leave a large scar?

No. Dr. Lokesh specializes in Minimal Access (Laparoscopic) and Laser Surgery. These advanced techniques use incredibly small incisions (often just millimeters wide), resulting in virtually invisible scarring and significantly reduced pain.

How fast is the recovery time?

Because we use minimally invasive techniques, tissue trauma is vastly reduced. Most pediatric patients are able to return home within 24 to 48 hours, and resume normal light activities within a few days.

Is anesthesia safe for my child?

Absolutely. We partner exclusively with specialized pediatric anesthesiologists. Your child's vitals are monitored continuously using state-of-the-art equipment to ensure maximum safety throughout the entire procedure.

What if we have an emergency after surgery?

You are never alone. Our clinical team provides 24/7 post-operative support. You will have direct access to our emergency line and WhatsApp channel for immediate guidance from Dr. Lokesh and his team.
